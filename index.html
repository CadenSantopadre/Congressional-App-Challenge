About FlightTally

FlightTally is a pilot's digital logbook, completely free of charge. We empower aviators with smart tracking tools to replace paper logs, track currency requirements, and visualize flight data effortlessly.

Why was it made?

FlightTally was built for the Congressional App Challenge. It was designed to help pilots ditch physical logbooks, minimize data errors, and provide highly accurate, reliable measurements for every hour in the sky
